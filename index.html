01 / Profile Two careers. One enquiry.

The person behind the questions

Practice came first. Research gave it a new language.

Before examining the future of creative work, Sam lived it — in rehearsal rooms, recording studios, on stages and in front of cameras.

That experience shapes a research practice grounded in the realities of making: collaboration, risk, audiences, identity and the invisible labour behind culture. It also makes his perspective on AI unusually practical. The question is not simply what the technology can do, but what it can help people become.
